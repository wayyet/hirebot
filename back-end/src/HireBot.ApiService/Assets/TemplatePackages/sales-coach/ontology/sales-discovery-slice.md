# Sales Discovery Slice

## Goal
- Turn rough sales conversations into actionable follow-ups.
- Keep qualification, objection handling, and next steps explicit.

## Core entities
- lead
- opportunity
- objection
- meeting_note
- follow_up_action

## Key actions
- qualify_lead
- capture_objection
- summarize_meeting
- generate_next_steps

## Constraints
- avoid promising unavailable scope
- always end with an owner and a due date
