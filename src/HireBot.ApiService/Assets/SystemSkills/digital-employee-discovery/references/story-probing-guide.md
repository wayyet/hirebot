# Story probing guide for NCrew discovery

Use this guide when the conversation starts sounding too abstract, too process-heavy, or too checklist-like.

The purpose of this guide is to pull the user back into a real scene and extract the hidden judgment logic inside human work.

## 1. What counts as a high-value scene

Look for moments with tension, ambiguity, or consequence, such as:
- a delay that caused business loss
- an escalation that should have happened earlier
- a wrong judgment or inconsistent judgment
- repeated manual review or back-and-forth
- a complaint from another team or customer
- a workaround that employees rely on but nobody designed
- a case where a strong employee handled it well but a weaker employee often fails

These are better than generic descriptions of the process.

## 2. Core pull-back questions

When the user is abstract, ask one of these:
- 最近一次这个场景真实发生是什么时候？
- 当时是谁接手的？
- 第一个动作是什么？
- 哪一步最麻烦？
- 一旦这里判断错了，后果是什么？

Do not ask all of them at once.
Pick the smallest question that gets the scene moving.

## 3. Five-step scene extraction

Once you have a real case, extract in this order:

### 1. Trigger
- what started the work?
- what made this case appear now?

### 2. Actor
- who took it first?
- who else touched it later?

### 3. Judgment
- where was the decision hard?
- what did the person need to notice?
- what would a weaker employee miss here?

### 4. Consequence
- what happens if the decision is wrong?
- what happens if it is slow?

### 5. Translation
Convert the scene into NCrew layers:
- digital employee: who should take this on?
- ontology slice: what must it understand?
- skills: what must it know how to do?
- CLI: what systems and tools must it touch?

## 4. Strong follow-up patterns

### Pattern A: inconsistency
- 你刚才说判断经常不一致。能举一个最近的例子吗？
- 当时两个不同的人，分别会怎么判断？
- 你觉得优秀的人会多看见什么？

### Pattern B: escalation risk
- 你刚才提到最怕漏掉。最近一次接近漏掉的情况是什么？
- 当时是靠什么被发现的？
- 如果没发现，会有什么后果？

### Pattern C: repetitive review
- 你刚才说反复要人工看。通常是因为哪一类信息不够？
- 这一步到底是在补数据，还是在做判断？
- 如果交给数字员工，哪部分你最放心，哪部分你最不放心？

### Pattern D: role modeling
- 在你们团队里，一个做得特别好的人，和普通人最大的区别是什么？
- 这个区别更像经验、规则意识，还是对风险的敏感度？
- 这里面哪些部分适合变成数字员工的能力边界？

## 5. What to listen for

Useful phrases from users often include:
- "总是"
- "经常"
- "每次都"
- "最怕"
- "最麻烦"
- "一旦漏掉"
- "只能靠某个人"
- "新人最容易错在"
- "老员工一眼就知道"

These are signs that hidden business logic is nearby.

## 6. Output effect

If you use this guide well, the final output becomes:
- more believable
- more anthropomorphic
- more grounded in real work
- more useful for defining the digital employee boundary

The user should feel:
- 你不是在套模板
- 你是真的理解了这份工作为什么难
- 你定义出来的数字员工像一个靠谱同事，而不是功能拼盘
